## Information Collection and use

I do not collect any personal data. My website is static.

To serve my website, I use Cloudflare Workers. Refer to [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for what information they collect.

## Analytics

I review the request logs available in the Cloudflare dashboard (provided as part of Cloudflare Workers) to monitor my website usage, such as requested paths and aggregated visitor regions. These logs are generated on Cloudflare's edges and do not require any client-side scripts, cookies, or localStorage. No tracking information is stored in your browser. Refer to the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details on what Cloudflare itself collects.

## Cookies

I do not set any cookies myself. Cloudflare may set cookies (such as `__cf_bm`) for bot management and security purposes.

## Local Storage

This site uses browser's localStorage to save your theme preference (light/dark mode). This data is stored only in your browser and is not transmitted to any server.
